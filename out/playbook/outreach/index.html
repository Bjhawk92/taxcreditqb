Outreach Playbook

Build support before the opening whistle.

The strongest development strategies begin before the formal matchup. Introduce the project, identify potential allies, build credible community support, and make it easier for the right voices to stand behind the deal.

Introduction and Outreach Emails

Coming to the Playbook

Municipal Introduction

An initial introduction to city managers, planning staff, housing officials, economic-development representatives, and elected officials.

Coming to the Playbook

Nonprofit Partner Introduction

Outreach to local nonprofit organizations whose mission, community knowledge, or services may align with the proposed development.

Coming to the Playbook

Community Organization Introduction

An introduction to neighborhood associations, chambers of commerce, civic organizations, faith-based groups, and other community stakeholders.

Coming to the Playbook

Service-Provider Outreach

Initial communication with organizations that may provide resident services, referrals, programming, or other support.

Coming to the Playbook

Employer and Institutional Outreach

Outreach to major employers, school districts, healthcare systems, colleges, and other institutions that understand local workforce and housing needs.

Coming to the Playbook

Financial and Development Partner Introduction

Professional introductions to lenders, syndicators, architects, contractors, consultants, and other potential members of the development team.

Coming to the Playbook

Meeting Follow-Up

Post-meeting emails that confirm what was discussed, document commitments, answer outstanding questions, and establish the next play.

Letters of Support

Coming to the Playbook

Municipal Letter of Support

Support from a mayor, city administrator, council member, planning official, housing department, or other local-government representative.

Coming to the Playbook

Housing Authority Letter of Support

Support addressing local housing needs, waiting lists, affordability gaps, or the proposed development’s role in the community.

Coming to the Playbook

Economic-Development Letter of Support

Support explaining how the development advances workforce housing, investment, redevelopment, or broader community objectives.

Coming to the Playbook

Nonprofit Letter of Support

Support from a local nonprofit based on community needs, the population it serves, or the development’s alignment with its mission.

Coming to the Playbook

Resident-Service Partner Letter

Support from an organization expected to provide services, education, health resources, transportation assistance, workforce programming, or resident referrals.

Coming to the Playbook

Neighborhood or Community Association Letter

Support from an organized neighborhood, homeowners association, civic group, or other community-based organization.

Coming to the Playbook

Chamber or Business Organization Letter

Support connecting the development to workforce recruitment, employee retention, economic growth, or local housing demand.

Coming to the Playbook

Major Employer Letter

Support describing the need for quality housing that is affordable to employees working in the market.

Coming to the Playbook

Healthcare or Educational Institution Letter

Support from hospitals, health systems, school districts, colleges, or universities identifying local housing needs among employees, students, families, or the broader community.

Coming to the Playbook

Faith-Based Organization Letter

Support from a church or faith-based organization familiar with local families, housing pressures, and community needs.

Coming to the Playbook

Social-Service Organization Letter

Support from organizations serving seniors, veterans, people with disabilities, families, or other populations relevant to the development.

Coming to the Playbook

Continuum of Care or Supportive-Housing Letter

Support for developments serving people experiencing homelessness or residents requiring coordinated services.

Coming to the Playbook

Development-Team Letter

Support from experienced architects, contractors, property managers, consultants, or financial partners confirming the team’s capacity and readiness.

Coming to the Playbook

General Community Letter of Support

A flexible framework for stakeholders who support the development but do not fit one of the more specialized categories.
