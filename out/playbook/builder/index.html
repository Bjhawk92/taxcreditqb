Presentation Builder

Bring us the deal. We’ll build the game plan.

Organize the facts, images, documents, audience details, and supporting evidence Tax Credit QB needs to understand the field and build the strongest case for your development. Work through the guided process at your own pace, save your progress, and submit everything when the assignment is ready to be scoped.

The Presentation Builder organizes your materials and prepares the assignment for review. Completing the builder does not automatically order a finished presentation. Tax Credit QB reviews the information, establishes the strategy, confirms the scope, fee, schedule, and deliverables, and then develops the final presentation.

A guided way to organize the assignment

Everything we need. Organized around your deal.

The Presentation Builder gives your team a structured process for assembling the information Tax Credit QB needs to understand the field and prepare the game plan. Clear prompts help organize the development facts, audience, objectives, supporting documents, visual assets, anticipated concerns, and requested outcome.

Tax Credit QB reviews the completed submission, identifies missing information, establishes the game plan, and confirms the scope before production begins. Nothing is published, presented as fact, or included in a final presentation without professional review.

  • Keep project information in one organized location
  • Separate public information from confidential materials
  • Identify the intended audience and desired outcome
  • Organize images and documents by purpose
  • Document known concerns and likely objections
  • Provide supporting evidence and source information
  • Review the completed Presentation Brief before submission
  • Reduce scattered emails and unidentified attachments

Scope and review

What happens after you submit.

  1. 01Tax Credit QB reviews the Presentation Brief and submitted materials.
  2. 02Missing information and strategic questions are identified.
  3. 03The scope, fee, schedule, and deliverables are confirmed.
  4. 04The client approves the assignment before production begins.
  5. 05Tax Credit QB develops the presentation.
  6. 06The client reviews the draft within the agreed revision allowance.
  7. 07Final presentation files are delivered.

Custom presentation work may involve additional research, document review, financial analysis, renderings, consultant coordination, meeting preparation, travel, or participation. These services are separately scoped unless expressly included in the proposal.
